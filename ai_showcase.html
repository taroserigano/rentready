Machine learning

Two calibrated model families

Gradient-boosted models trained on a five-year rent-ledger schema. Strong discrimination and tight calibration — the probabilities mean what they say.

Read these numbers carefully. All training and evaluation data is SYNTHETIC, generated by a parametric simulator (generate_residents.py), and the metrics are measured on a held-out split of that same generator. They show the pipeline reliably recovers a known data-generating process — not real-world predictive skill. Two features are near-exact algebraic inverses of latent terms in the simulator's own equation. Measured against naive baselines, the arrears heads are worse than assuming the balance never changes on 4 of 5 targets (model R² 0.89 vs persistence 0.96 at one quarter), and p_90d_12m's flattering 0.011 ECE matches a predict-the-base-rate baseline exactly. Only peak_balance_12m clearly beats its baseline.

Responsible by construction

Decision-support, not automated decisions

Fairness and safety are architectural, not afterthoughts — and verified live.

✓Never decides

Estimates inform human outreach and retention — the system never approves, denies, evicts, or prices.

✓Protected classes excluded

Race, national origin, sex, disability, age, and familial status are structurally excluded from every model input.

✓Human review routing

Serious-delinquency flags route to a person for supportive review — never an automated adverse action.

✓Adversarially hardened

Prompt-injection attempts and protected-class inference are declined — confirmed in live testing.
